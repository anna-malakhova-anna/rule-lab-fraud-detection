Same team, different order

Every alert becomes a case: one per business per review day. Each case gets a priority score: what is at stake (the payment, the application's stated revenue, or the credit line) times how often that rule's alerts turned out to be fraud in the tuning months, as known at the time. Neither part reads the answer key. The team works 90 cases per business day. A hold that nobody reviews within two business days lapses and the money moves.

Analyst decisions become labels

Reviewed cases are labelled on the day of the decision, with error: analysts clear 10% of fraud cases by mistake and mark 2% of good ones as fraud. Cases nobody reviewed are labelled only when the fraud is confirmed some other way. For noisy rules, analyst error inflates measured precision far more than label delay deflates it.
