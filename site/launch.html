Launch review

Instant payouts, launched 01 Mar 2026

Controls | Threat model | Rule blind spots

From the launch date, businesses can send payouts that arrive in seconds. That's a feature for honest customers, and for fraudsters it removes the time between the money leaving and anyone noticing. The rules here score a payment after it is sent; on a standard payment there is still time to hold it, on an instant one there isn't. Fraudsters in the simulation adapt: most cash-outs switch to instant, and big ones are split under the per-payout cap.

Controls to build in before launch

Each control is a check made before the payout is sent. When it trips, the payout simply goes the standard way, which gives the other rules their window back. Nobody is refused, but the customer waits about a day. Net value prices each good business slowed down like any good business flagged ($150, the stated cost in config/backtest.yaml).
