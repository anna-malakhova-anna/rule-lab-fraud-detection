Quarterly review, after 2026-Q2

A rule is a retire candidate when it adds no fraud dollars on top of the other rules for 2 reviews in a row. That is leave-one-out: run the rules without it and see what is lost. Catching a case earlier counts, so a rule can be worth keeping with no case to itself. It is flagged to retune when its precision is below its break-even (the precision at which its false alarms cost as much as it saves), and for drift when its volume doubles or halves against the quarter before. Fraud confirmed months later (mule accounts, credit bust-outs) is judged on an older quarter of alerts, so a missing label isn't mistaken for a false alarm.
