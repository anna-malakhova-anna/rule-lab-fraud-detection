How the month-12 pattern was found

Unauthorized returns on ACH debits had been near zero, then jumped. The first alarm became visible on 22 Dec 2025. The investigation (docs/investigations/INV-001-ach-debit-returns.md, frozen at Phase 2) led to R9, which now runs in the champion.
