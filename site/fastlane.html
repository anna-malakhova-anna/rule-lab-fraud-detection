Fast lane

Less friction for established, clean customers

Where alerts are false | The trade-off | What gets through | Onboarding

A business is in the fast lane while it has been open at least 365 days and has had no returned item in the last 180. For those businesses, the rules aimed at a fraudster's own account (R1, R2, R9) send a document request instead of a hold, on amounts below a cap. Rules that protect a victim, and the credit rules, keep their action: established, clean businesses are exactly who takeovers, payment redirection and bust-outs target.

The trade-off, by cap

The cap is swept, not tuned. Under the stated costs a hold is priced only by the money it ties up, so net value can't choose it; the number to judge is the fraud let through for each hold avoided. The training months would have pointed to a higher cap than the test months support.
